# NSCET AI College Assistant — User Manual

Welcome to **NSCET AI Assistant**, the 24/7 virtual college assistant for **Nadar Saraswathi College of Engineering & Technology (NSCET)**, Vadapudupatti, Theni, Tamil Nadu.

---

## 1. For Prospective Students, Parents & Visitors

1. **Ask Questions Anytime (English or தமிழ்):**
   - Open the **AI Chat Assistant** view.
   - Click any **Suggested Question** or type your own question in English or Tamil (toggle the **தமிழ்** button for sample Tamil prompts).
   - Select your visitor role in the top header (`Prospective Student` or `Parent / Visitor`) so responses match your context.
2. **Explore Campus & Departments:**
   - Visit **College Information** for campus facilities (Labs, Library, Transport, Hostel, Clubs) and office contacts.
   - Visit **Departments (8)** to browse CSE, AI&DS, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, and Science & Humanities.
3. **Register an Admission Enquiry:**
   - Open **Admission Assistant**, review UG/PG programmes and the required certificates checklist, and submit the **Admission Enquiry Registration Form**.
   - Save your generated reference code (`NSCET-ADM-YYYY-XXXX`) for follow-up with the NSCET Admission Office.
4. **Rate Responses:**
   - Below every assistant answer, select **Helpful** or **Not helpful** (and optionally add a short note) to help improve accuracy.

---

## 2. For Current NSCET Students

1. Click **Portal Sign In** in the top-right header and sign in with your Student account (or register a new student account via the **Student Register** tab).
2. Open **Student Academics** to access authenticated academic guidance (Regulations, Syllabus, CIA Assessments, University Examination Guidelines, Learning Resources, and student-level documents).
3. Visit **Placement Assistant** for aptitude, resume, and technical interview preparation frameworks.
4. Your chat history is linked to your account across sessions, and student-scoped RAG documents automatically become searchable in your chats.

---

## 3. For NSCET Faculty Members

1. Sign in via **Portal Sign In** with your Faculty credentials.
2. Open **Faculty Assistant** to access FDP, NPTEL, and Research guidance plus faculty-scoped documents.
3. Use the **Academic Documentation Draft Assistant** to generate structured templates for:
   - Course Lesson Plan Outlines
   - Workshop / Seminar Circulars
   - Bloom’s Taxonomy Question Paper Blueprints

---

## 4. For College Administrators

1. Sign in with your **Admin** account to unlock the **Administration** sidebar section.
2. **RAG Documents Manager (`RAG Documents`):**
   - Upload official NSCET PDF, DOCX, TXT, or MD documents (Brochures, Academic Regulations, Syllabus, Admission Guidelines, Placement Reports, Circulars).
   - Set the appropriate **Category** and **Role Access Level** (`public`, `student`, `faculty`, or `admin`), and mark verified college files as **Verified Official**.
   - Inspect extracted chunks, replace outdated files, delete obsolete files, and test role-filtered retrieval using the **RAG Semantic Search & RBAC Tester**.
3. **Admin Dashboard (`Admin Dashboard`):**
   - **Analytics Overview:** Track total users, conversations, daily enquiries, admission leads, helpful rate, top FAQs, and **Unanswered Questions** (use unanswered queries to decide which official PDFs to upload next).
   - **Admission Leads:** Update lead status (`New` → `Contacted` → `Counselled` → `Admitted` / `Closed`).
   - **Feedback Reports:** Review user ratings and comments.
   - **User Management:** Create Faculty/Admin/Student accounts and activate or disable access.
   - **Audit Logs:** Review chronological security and administrative events.
